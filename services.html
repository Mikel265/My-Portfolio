Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about project timelines, file formats, and terms.

You will receive full source files and exported formats tailored for print and web: Vector AI/EPS, Photoshop PSD, PDF, PNG (transparent backgrounds), SVG, and high-res JPEG files.

Posters, social media graphics, and flyers take 24–48 hours. Logo designs and brand identity packages take 3–7 business days depending on revision iterations.

Each package includes dedicated revision rounds (2 to unlimited depending on package). We review feedback collaboratively to refine typography, color tweaks, and layout adjustments until you are 100% satisfied.

Projects typically require a 50% deposit before kickoff and 50% upon final artwork approval before master file handover. Payments are accepted via M-PESA, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
